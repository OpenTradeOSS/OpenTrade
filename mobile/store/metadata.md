## Description

OpenTrade runs AI trading agents for you, and keeps you in charge of every order.

You give each agent a strategy on the web. It watches the market, does its research and, when it wants to trade, asks you first. OpenTrade on your iPhone is where those requests land.

• Approvals: get a notification the moment an agent wants to place an order. See the symbol, side, size, limit price and estimated cost, then approve with Face ID or reject. Orders you don't approve in time are refused.
• Agents: see which agents are working, idle, or waiting on you.
• Portfolio: account value, today's change, buying power and positions from your connected broker.
• Activity: every proposed order, decision and fill, in order.

OpenTrade is software, not a broker or investment adviser. Orders go to the brokerage account you connect, under your own account and its terms. Trading involves risk, including loss of principal. AI agents make mistakes; that's why every order waits for you.

OpenTrade is open source: github.com/OpenTradeOSS/OpenTrade

## App Review notes

OpenTrade is a companion app for OpenTrade Cloud, a service that runs AI trading agents in
a private cloud workspace per user. The iPhone app is for reviewing and approving the
orders those agents propose, and for monitoring them. It does not sell anything: plans and
credits are managed on the web, and no purchase links appear in the app.

Demo account (credentials in the review form). It has two agents. To see an approval:
the demo workspace raises a sample order request for review; open the Approvals tab. If
none is pending when you look, pull to refresh after a minute. Approving or rejecting
it has no effect outside the demo, as no brokerage account is connected to it.

Account deletion: Account tab → Delete account.
Face ID is used only to confirm an approval.
